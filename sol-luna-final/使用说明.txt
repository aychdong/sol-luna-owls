安装 / INSTALL
1. 先备份旧版 sol、luna 文件夹。Back up existing sol and luna folders.
2. 在 Finder 按 ⌘⇧G，输入 ~/.codex/pets/
   In Finder, press ⌘⇧G and open ~/.codex/pets/
3. 复制「可直接安装」里的 sol、luna 两个文件夹进去，不要复制外层目录。
   Copy the two sol and luna folders from 可直接安装, without their enclosing folder.
4. 在应用宠物界面刷新并重新选择；未更新时完整退出再打开应用。
   Refresh and reselect the pet. Fully quit and reopen the app if necessary.

每只宠物只有 pet.json 和 spritesheet.webp 两个文件；无需运行脚本。
Each pet contains pet.json and spritesheet.webp. No scripts are needed.
